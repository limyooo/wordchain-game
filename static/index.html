    🔗 About the game

    WordChain is an AI-powered English word chain game for anyone who wants to build their vocabulary. Take turns with an AI partner and see how long you can keep the chain going!

    🎮 How to play

    The AI starts with a word, like “apple”. Reply with a word that starts with its last letter (“egg”), then the AI continues from your word’s last letter (“goat”), and so on. Each word must be a real English word and can only be used once per game.

    ✨ Features

    • Instant rule checks: wrong first letters and repeated words are caught right away
    • Smart AI partner: the AI picks words that keep the game fun and challenging
    • Helpful hints: stuck? Ask for a hint instead of giving up
    • Saved games: your history is saved, so you can return to any game

    👥 Great for

    • English learners
    • Vocabulary practice
    • Warming up a classroom
    • Quick word games with friends
