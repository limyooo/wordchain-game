    🔗 About the game

    WordChain is an AI-powered English word chain game for anyone who wants to build their vocabulary. Take turns with an AI partner and see how long you can keep the chain going!

    🎮 How to play

    The AI starts with a word, like “apple”. Reply with a word that starts with its last letter (“egg”), then the AI continues from your word’s last letter (“goat”), and so on. Each word must be a real English word, at least 3 letters long, and can only be used once per game.

    Answer before the timer runs out. Keep the chain going for enough rounds and you win!

    🏠 Classroom rooms

    The host creates a room and shares the 4-letter code. Everyone joins with a nickname, the host clicks Start, and the leaderboard shows who lasts the longest.

    ✨ Features

    • Instant rule checks: wrong first letters, made-up words and repeats are caught right away
    • Smart AI partner: the AI picks words that keep the game fun and challenging
    • Timed turns: beat the clock on every turn
    • Your difficulty: choose how many rounds to win and how many seconds per turn
    • Live leaderboard: compete with your whole class

    Keep the chain going and learn new words along the way!
